Statistics

What Philippine surveys and police records say, and what the numbers cannot show.

These numbers come from the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) and from police records. They show that VAWC is a real and continuing problem, but each number measures something different, so read the labels before comparing them.

14.0%
of women aged 15 to 49 experienced physical or sexual violence in the 2022 National Demographic and Health Survey (NDHS), based on 19,228 women.[2]

Other figures

26%
of ever-married women aged 15 to 49 had experienced physical, sexual or emotional violence from a husband or partner, in the 2017 NDHS. By type: 20% emotional, 14% physical and 5% sexual. Divorced, separated or widowed women reported more (53%) than women who were married or living together (24%).[3]
3,300+
VAWC-related calls were received by the National 911 Operations and Coordinating Center from January 2025 to February 2026.[8]

How to read these numbers

  • The 2017 figure covers ever-married women and violence by a husband or partner. The 2022 figure covers all women aged 15 to 49 and only physical or sexual violence. They should not be compared directly.[2][3]
  • Survey numbers depend on women being willing to answer. Police numbers depend on women reporting. Both can be lower than what really happens.
  • Behind every number is a person. This site uses them to show scale, not to rank suffering.
