Warning Signs

A private checklist for noticing abuse in a relationship. Nothing you tick is saved.

Private by design. Nothing you tick is saved, sent or counted anywhere. Close the page when you are finished, or use Leave this site.

Tick anything that has happened in a relationship, yours or someone else's. These signs can appear in any relationship, including between partners of the same sex. This is a way to notice patterns. It is not a diagnosis.

Control
Words and fear
Money
Physical and sexual harm

Why these signs matter

The behaviors in this list match acts that RA 9262 names: threats, harassment, stalking, controlling a woman's movements, cutting off financial support, and physical or sexual harm.[1] One sign alone does not always mean abuse, but a pattern of control and fear deserves attention.

You can use this same list to understand what you are seeing. For how to talk to them, go to Helping a friend.
