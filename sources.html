Sources

References for every law, number and contact on this site.

Every law, number and contact on this site is listed here. The small bracketed numbers on other pages, like [1], link to the matching entry below.

Check before you rely on it. Laws can be amended, statistics are updated, and hotline numbers change. The information was gathered in October 2026. Please confirm important details on the official websites.

  1. Republic Act No. 9262, Anti-Violence Against Women and Their Children Act of 2004 (full text). https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/22128Publisher: Supreme Court E-Library. Used for: Definition of VAWC, who is covered, forms of abuse, protection orders (BPO 15 days, TPO 30 days, PPO), who may file, public crime, no compromise.
  2. Philippine Statistics Authority. 2024 Fact Sheet on Women and Men in the Philippines: Violence Against Women (2022 NDHS; PNP data as of February 2024). https://psa.gov.ph/sites/default/files/phdsd/Violence%20Against%20Women%202024%20Fact%20Sheet%20on%20Women%20and%20Men.pdfPublisher: PSA. Used for: Survey percentages (14.0%, 9.8%, 1.5%, 2.7%, by age) and PNP-recorded VAW cases, 2021 to 2023.
  3. Philippine Statistics Authority. One In Four Women Have Ever Experienced Spousal Violence (preliminary results, 2017 NDHS). https://psa.gov.ph/statistics/national-demographic-health-survey/node/129908Publisher: PSA. Used for: 2017 spousal violence figures (26%, 20%, 14%, 5%, 53% and 24%).
  4. Department of Social Welfare and Development. DSWD urges public to “break the silence” by reporting cases of violence against women. https://dswd.gov.ph/?p=107885Publisher: DSWD. Used for: Ways to report VAWC: 911, Aleng Pulis hotline, Barangay VAW Desk and PNP Women and Children Protection Desk.
  5. Department of Social Welfare and Development. Service providers for victim-survivors of violence against women and children. https://www.dswd.gov.ph/?p=74649Publisher: DSWD. Used for: DSWD shelters and protective custody, NBI VAWC desk and NCMH crisis hotline listings. This page dates from the COVID-19 period, so confirm numbers before use.
  6. Commission on Human Rights. Statement condemning the killings of two women and a child in Las Piñas City. https://chr.gov.ph/statement-of-the-commission-on-human-rights-condemning-the-killings-of-two-women-and-a-child-in-las-pinas-city/Publisher: CHR.   7. Philippine Information Agency. Reporting violence against women and children (VAWC) (March 19, 2024), citing the Philippine Commission on Women. https://pia.gov.ph/features/2024/03/19/reporting-violence-against-women-and-children-vawcPublisher: PIA. Used for: Women and Children Protection Units in DOH hospitals and other reporting channels.
  8. National 911 Center Records Over 3,300 VAWC Calls in 14 Months (March 27, 2026). https://nga911.com/blogs/post/national-911-center-records-over-3300-vawc-calls-14-monthsPublisher: NGA911PH. Used for: Number of VAWC-related calls to 911, January 2025 to February 2026.
